# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the
Darkhorse Trading community a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes: using welcoming and inclusive language; being
respectful of differing viewpoints; gracefully accepting constructive
criticism; focusing on what is best for the community; showing empathy.

Unacceptable behavior includes: sexualized language or imagery; trolling,
insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks; public or
private harassment; publishing others' private information (doxxing); any
conduct which would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
setting; **promoting trading services, deposit links, or "guaranteed profit"
schemes** (instant ban -- this project's entire purpose is the honest
counter-example).

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to the maintainer via GitHub
(all reports are reviewed confidentially). The maintainer may comment, warn,
temporarily or permanently ban any contributor whose behavior violates this
code.
